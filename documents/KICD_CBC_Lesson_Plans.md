# LESSON 1: INTRODUCTION TO BODY SYSTEMS

## SPECIFIC LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end of the lesson, the learner should be able to:
- Identify the major parts of the human circulatory system
- Describe the functions of the heart and blood vessels
- Appreciate the importance of the circulatory system in daily life

## CORE COMPETENCIES

- **Communication and Collaboration** – explaining ideas orally
- **Critical Thinking and Problem Solving** – linking structure to function
- **Digital Literacy** – interacting with VR/AR learning tools

## VALUES

- Responsibility
- Self-awareness
- Healthy living

## KEY INQUIRY QUESTION

**Why is the heart important for human survival?**

## LEARNING RESOURCES

- Tablet (NIKOlearn app preloaded)
- VR headset (shared)
- Charts / digital diagrams of the heart
- Local offline server (content sync)

## LESSON STRUCTURE

### INTRODUCTION (5 Minutes)

**Teacher asks learners:**
- "Where do you think blood comes from?"
- "What happens when the heart stops beating?"

Learners share responses.

**Teacher introduces the topic:** The Human Circulatory System.

### LESSON DEVELOPMENT

#### Step 1: Exploration (10 Minutes)

- Learners watch a short animated introduction on NIKOlearn
- AR overlay displays heart location on the human body
- Teacher guides discussion on observed parts

#### Step 2: Concept Development (15 Minutes)

- One learner at a time experiences the VR heart tour
- Others observe via tablet mirroring
- **Teacher explains:**
  - Heart chambers
  - Blood movement
- Learners ask questions

#### Step 3: Application (5 Minutes)

- Learners identify activities that make the heart beat faster
- Short group discussion

### CONCLUSION (5 Minutes)

**Learners recap:**
- What the heart does
- Why it is important

**Teacher summarizes key points.**

## ASSESSMENT

- Oral questions
- Identification of heart parts
- Observation checklist

## REMEDIAL ACTIVITIES

- One-on-one guidance using diagrams
- Replay VR experience at slower pace

## EXTENSION ACTIVITIES

- Learners research heart-healthy foods at home

# LESSON 2: BLOOD AND BLOOD VESSELS

## SPECIFIC LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end of the lesson, the learner should be able to:
- Name types of blood vessels
- Describe the function of each blood vessel
- Explain the direction of blood flow

## CORE COMPETENCIES

- Critical Thinking
- Communication
- Digital Literacy

## KEY INQUIRY QUESTION

**How does blood move around the body?**

## LEARNING RESOURCES

- NIKOlearn interactive diagrams
- VR blood flow simulation
- AR vein visualization

### INTRODUCTION (5 Minutes)

- Teacher asks learners to feel their pulse
- Learners describe what they feel

### LESSON DEVELOPMENT

#### Step 1: Exploration (10 Minutes)

- Learners view interactive diagram:
  - Arteries
  - Veins
  - Capillaries

#### Step 2: Experiential Learning (15 Minutes)

- VR simulation shows blood flowing through vessels
- Teacher explains oxygenated vs deoxygenated blood

#### Step 3: Application (5 Minutes)

- Learners match vessels to functions in the app

### CONCLUSION (5 Minutes)

- Learners explain blood flow in their own words

## ASSESSMENT

- Digital quiz (5 questions)
- Oral explanation

# LESSON 3: HEALTHY LIVING & LIFESTYLE

## SPECIFIC LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the end of the lesson, the learner should be able to:
- Identify healthy habits for the heart
- Explain the effects of unhealthy lifestyles
- Demonstrate responsibility for personal health

## CORE COMPETENCIES

- Self-efficacy
- Citizenship
- Critical Thinking

## KEY INQUIRY QUESTION

**How can our daily choices affect our heart?**

## LEARNING RESOURCES

- Scenario-based simulations
- Health checklist (digital)
- Family Mode dashboard

### INTRODUCTION (5 Minutes)

**Teacher presents a scenario:**
"A child eats junk food every day. What might happen?"

### LESSON DEVELOPMENT

#### Step 1: Exploration (10 Minutes)

- Learners interact with health scenarios
- AR shows blocked vs healthy arteries

#### Step 2: Guided Discussion (15 Minutes)

**Teacher explains:**
- Balanced diet
- Exercise
- Avoiding harmful substances

#### Step 3: Application (5 Minutes)

- Learners make a personal health pledge in NIKOlearn

### CONCLUSION (5 Minutes)

- Learners share one healthy habit they will practice

## ASSESSMENT

- Reflection response
- Observation of participation

## PARENT INVOLVEMENT

- Learners share lesson summary at home
- Parents monitor progress via Family Mode
